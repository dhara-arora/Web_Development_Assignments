Explore Shimla — Queen of the Hills

Nestled in the pristine foothill ranges of the Himalayas, Shimla offers breathtaking mountain vistas, rich colonial architecture, and vibrant cultural markets. Whether you seek peaceful alpine walks or rich historical exploration, this destination caters to every discerning traveler.

Travel Advisory: Peak tourist season runs from March to June, while December to February offers magical snowfall experiences. Average seasonal temperature ranges from 8°C to 24°C.

Top Recommended Experiences & Attractions

Explore iconic destinations that showcase the regional culture, heritage, and natural beauty:

  • The Mall Road: The heart of the town featuring historic cafes, local handicraft shops, and vibrant pedestrian walkways.
  • Jakhoo Temple & Hill: Situated at 2,455 meters, home to the ancient Hanuman temple and panoramic mountain views.
  • Christ Church: The second oldest church in Northern India, famous for its neo-Gothic architecture and stained-glass windows.
  • Kalka–Shimla Toy Train: A UNESCO World Heritage railway delivering a scenic Journey through 102 tunnels and over 800 bridges.

Recommended 1-Day Heritage Itinerary

Maximize your time with this curated step-by-step day plan:

  1. 08:00 AM: Start with breakfast at a local heritage bakery along The Mall Road.
  2. 09:30 AM: Take the Jakhoo Ropeway up to Jakhoo Temple for morning mountain views.
  3. 12:00 PM: Visit Christ Church and explore the colonial architecture around the Ridge.
  4. 02:00 PM: Enjoy traditional Himachali cuisine at a local restaurant.
  5. 04:00 PM: Tour the Viceregal Lodge (Rashtrapati Niwas) and botanical gardens.
  6. 06:30 PM: Evening stroll and souvenir shopping at Lakkar Bazaar.
