Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is the country's premier gathering for students, recent graduates, and tech professionals. Explore career pathways in Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, and Emerging Technologies across 100+ companies.

Experience interactive workshops, live pitch competitions, and exclusive networking sessions designed to accelerate professional growth. Admission is free, but registration is strictly mandatory.

Preparation Tips for Attendees

Follow these essential guidelines to maximize your experience during the 2-day event:

  • Resume Preparation: Bring at least 10 printed copies of your updated resume and update your digital profile link.
  • Portfolio Ready: Host your software projects or design portfolios on GitHub or personal domain links.
  • Elevator Pitch: Practice a 30-second introduction highlighting your academic background, skills, and career goals.
  • Dress Code: Business casual attire is recommended for interviews and corporate booth visits.
  • Questions Prepared: Prepare 2–3 thoughtful questions for company recruiters regarding culture and growth.

Attendee Journey — 5 Step Guide

Follow this step-by-step roadmap from registration to post-expo follow-up:

  1. Register online through the official portal form below to receive your digital entry QR pass.
  2. Check in at Registration Desk A on Day 1 to collect your attendee badge and welcome kit.
  3. Attend morning keynote sessions and interactive workshops in Hall 1 & Hall 2.
  4. Visit corporate exhibition booths to network with talent acquisition teams and submit resumes.
  5. Follow up with contacts made during the expo within 48 hours via professional email or networking channels.

Expo Concepts & Terminology

Career Expo
A centralized event where employers, recruiters, and educational institutions meet with prospective job seekers to discuss career opportunities.
Internship
A professional learning experience that offers meaningful, practical work related to a student's field of study or career interest.
Placement
Direct hiring recruitment processes conducted by organizations to onboard graduating students into full-time employment roles.
Workshop
An interactive practical training session focused on developing specific technical, analytical, or soft skills.
Certification
A credential awarded by industry bodies verifying an individual's mastery in specific technologies or tools.
Networking
The process of establishing and nurturing professional relationships to share knowledge, opportunities, and advice.
