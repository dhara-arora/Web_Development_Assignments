Welcome to Apex Heights, an elite high-rise residential sanctuary designed for modern living. Nestled in a prime urban corridor, this development spans across 12.5 acres with over 70% open green space, panoramic city views, and state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Key Feature: Energy-efficient architecture certified with a Platinum Green Building rating, reducing environmental footprint while cutting utility costs by > 25&percent;.

Property Acquisition Steps

Follow our streamlined 5-step home buying process for a seamless ownership journey:

  1. Site Visit & Unit Selection: Tour sample flats and select your desired apartment configuration.
  2. Token Booking & Application: Pay the 10&percent; initial booking amount and submit KYC documents.
  3. Home Loan Sanction: Coordinate with our banking partners for quick home loan approvals.
  4. Allotment & Agreement Registration: Sign the formal agreement for sale under municipal regulatory norms.
  5. Handover & Possession: Complete final payment schedule, inspect premises, and collect physical keys.
