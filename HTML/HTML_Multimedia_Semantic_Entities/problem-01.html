Welcome to Apex Global University

Apex Global University is a premier higher education institution dedicated to nurturing critical thinking, advanced scientific research, and professional excellence. Established with a vision to integrate world-class pedagogy with hands-on practice, our campus provides an inclusive environment for students across the globe.

Campus Highlights: Spanning over 150 acres, our campus features state-of-the-art research centers, high-performance computing labs, and comprehensive sports infrastructure.

Key Campus Facilities

  • Central Digital Library: 24/7 access to over 500,000 journals, e-books, and research archives.
  • Advanced Innovation Labs: High-performance computing clusters and robotics development centers.
  • Residential Hostels: Fully furnished, Wi-Fi enabled living quarters with dedicated dining halls.
  • Sports Complex: Olympic-standard swimming pool, indoor badminton courts, and athletics tracks.

Step-by-Step Admission Process Guide

Follow this step-by-step roadmap to complete your admission journey smoothly:

  1. Fill out and submit the online application form before the cutoff date.
  2. Upload scanned marksheets, government photo ID, and passport-size photographs.
  3. Pay the non-refundable application processing fee of ₹ 1,000 online.
  4. Check the portal regularly for merit list publication and interview call letters.
  5. Report for physical document verification and fee payment to secure your provisional admission seat.
