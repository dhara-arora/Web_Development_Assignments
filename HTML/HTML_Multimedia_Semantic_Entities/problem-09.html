Essential Personal Finance Tips

  • Emergency Reserve: Maintain 3–6 months of essential living expenses in liquid cash.
  • Automate Investments: Set up monthly auto-debits into diversified mutual funds or index funds.
  • Monitor Credit Score: Review your annual credit report regularly to audit for errors or unauthorized inquiries.
  • Avoid Unsecured High-Interest Debt: Prioritize clearing high-yield personal loans first.

Account Opening Procedure — 5 Steps

  1. Select the financial product (Savings, Fixed Deposit, or Credit Card) matching your goal.
  2. Verify basic eligibility criteria including minimum age (≥ 18 years) and income proof.
  3. Complete digital identity verification (KYC) using official government credentials.
  4. Submit your initial account funding amount via online bank transfer or UPI.
  5. Activate your mobile banking credentials and online debit/credit card access.

Financial Terminology & Key Metrics

Interest Rate
The annualized percentage rate (%) charged by lenders or paid by banks to depositors on total funds.
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)
A fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month.
Principal
The original sum of money borrowed in a loan or put into an investment, separate from compounding interest.
Tenure
The predetermined duration or time period over which a loan is repaid or an investment matures.
