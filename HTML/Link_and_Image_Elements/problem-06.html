TechFest 2026: Innovate The Future

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is officially returning as the flagship annual technology festival, bringing together over 3,000 student developers, innovators, and tech enthusiasts from across the nation. Experience three action-packed days filled with collaborative coding hackathons, robotic showcases, and technical workshops led by industry leaders.

Highlights

Participants will engage in 48-hour competitive hackathons, algorithmic problem-solving challenges, and web application showcases. Beyond coding competitions, the festival features hands-on hardware exhibits, artificial intelligence demonstrations, and interactive project booths designed to test technical skills in real time.

Date & Venue

Event Dates: November 14 — November 16, 2026
Main Venue: Main University Auditorium & Tech Block Labs
Location: GLA University Campus, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh — 201306
Timing: 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM IST Daily

Speakers

We are thrilled to present keynote addresses from prominent software architects, startup founders, and research engineers. Featured guest speakers will discuss machine learning breakthroughs, scalable cloud infrastructure, open-source development practices, and career navigation in modern software engineering.

Registration

Registration is open to all undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students. Early registration guarantees access to specialized tech workshops, delegate kits, and competition entry slots.

Important Notice: The final deadline for online team registration is October 31, 2026.
