About Us

Founded in 2021, The Artisan Bean Café brings world-class specialty coffee and handcrafted delicacies to the neighborhood. We source 100% organic, ethically harvested Arabica beans and roast them locally to bring out distinct flavor profiles. Whether you are looking for a quiet spot to study, meet friends, or start your morning, our doors are always open.

Signature Items

Our menu highlights local ingredients paired with expert brewing techniques:

• Signature Velvet Espresso: A rich, double-shot espresso layered with silky steamed milk and subtle notes of dark chocolate.
• Artisanal Butter Croissant: Flaky, golden-brown layers baked fresh every morning using traditional French butter recipes.
• Avocado & Poached Egg Toast: Freshly smashed avocado served on sourdough toast, topped with organic poached eggs and chili flakes.
