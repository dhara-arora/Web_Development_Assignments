University Secures $5 Million National AI Research Grant

Groundbreaking funding will accelerate ethical artificial intelligence and machine learning initiatives across engineering departments.

GLA University has officially been awarded a $5 million federal research grant to establish a state-of-the-art Artificial Intelligence Excellence Center. This major achievement marks one of the largest academic technology grants received by the institution in over a decade, positioning the university as a leading hub for technological innovation and advanced algorithmic research.

The initiative was formally announced at the main campus assembly hall located in Greater Noida on August 25, 2026, before an audience of faculty members, student researchers, and industry representatives. University leaders confirmed that the funds will be allocated immediately toward acquiring high-performance GPU computing clusters and expanding undergraduate research fellowships.

The grant was awarded following a competitive evaluation process spanning over six months, where university faculty demonstrated exceptional promise in autonomous systems and data science. Academic committee chairs emphasized that integrating ethical AI principles directly into engineering curricula was the decisive factor that set the university's proposal apart from competing submissions nationwide.

With project deployment scheduled for early next month, over 1,200 engineering students will gain direct access to specialized research hardware and mentorship programs. University directors noted that building strong partnerships between student developers and industry leaders remains essential to solving real-world challenges in healthcare, logistics, and sustainable urban infrastructure.
