5. Admissions & Sequential Process

Applicants must complete the following mandatory steps in sequence to secure admission:

  1. Review course eligibility requirements and fee structure detailed below.
  2. Complete the online admission registration form with accurate personal and academic data.
  3. Upload clear scanned copies of all required certificates and identification documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable registration application fee online.
  5. Download the generated application acknowledgement slip with your reference ID.
  6. Appear for the university entrance test or merit counseling session on the assigned date.

6. Admission Terminology & Key Definitions

Eligibility
The mandatory educational background, minimum aggregate percentage, and age criteria needed for course enrollment.
Merit List
The rank order list published by the university based on academic cutoff percentage or entrance exam scores.
Counselling
The formal seat allocation process where verified applicants select their preferred course branch according to merit rank.
Scholarship
Financial aid grants rewarded to high-achieving students based on merit rank, athletic achievements, or financial need.
Admission Number
A permanent unique identification number assigned to a candidate upon successfully completing enrollment.
