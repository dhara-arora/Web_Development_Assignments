3. Medical Visit Terminology

New Patient
A patient registering with Aapex Care Medical Clinic for the first time or seeking treatment for a new health condition.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation to review ongoing medical treatment, lab test results, or recovery progress from a prior visit.
Consultation
A formal clinical evaluation between a specialist doctor and a patient to discuss medical concerns and diagnosis.
Emergency
Urgent medical situations requiring immediate clinical intervention and critical treatment.
Teleconsultation
A remote video or audio medical consultation conducted securely online for non-emergency healthcare needs.

4. Appointment Guidelines & Required Documents

Step-by-Step Appointment Process

  1. Review doctor availability and fee schedules detailed in the table above.
  2. Fill in the complete online patient intake form below.
  3. Select your preferred consultation type, specialty, and appointment date/time.
  4. Receive an SMS and email booking confirmation containing your tracking ID.
  5. Report to the clinic reception 15 minutes prior to your scheduled time slot.

Information & Documents to Keep Ready

  • Government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Previous medical records, prescription history, and lab diagnostic reports.
  • Existing Patient ID card (if you are a returning patient).
  • Health insurance policy card or membership details (if applicable).
  • List of current medications and active drug allergies.
