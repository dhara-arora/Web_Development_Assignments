Event Overview & Key Objectives

The National Career & Education Expo 2026 is India's largest flagship event bringing together top universities, multinational corporations, tech startups, and government research bodies under one roof. Whether you are seeking undergraduate admissions, higher research grants, internships, or full-time corporate placements, this event offers direct access to key decision-makers.

Our core mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations by facilitating direct interactions, workshops, and recruitment drives over three dedicated exhibition days.

Important Entry Requirement: Entry is free for pre-registered candidates, but all attendees must present their digital pass barcode upon arrival at Hall NO 4*.

Preparation Guide & Attendee Journey

Essential Preparation Tips for Attendees

  • Update Documents: Carry at least 5 printed copies of your updated CV or resume.
  • Digital Credentials: Keep a working digital link to your GitHub, LinkedIn, or portfolio ready.
  • Dress Professionally: Business casual or formal attire is highly recommended for company interviews.
  • Research Exhibitors: Review the exhibitor directory table above in advance to prioritize booth visits.

The 5-Step Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration on this portal to obtain your unique Registration Pass.
  2. Check in at the venue registration counter on event day for quick badge printing.
  3. Attend relevant keynotes and workshops listed in the official schedule matrix.
  4. Engage directly with university reps and company recruiters at their respective booths.
  5. Submit your physical resume or digital QR pass for immediate interview scheduling.

Terminology & Frequently Asked Questions

Key Career Terminology

Career Fair
An organized event where employers and academic institutions meet prospective candidates and students.
Internship
A temporary position emphasizing supervised hands-on learning and practical work experience.
Placement
The formal recruitment process culminating in a permanent job offer from an hiring company.
Scholarship
Financial grant awarded to students to support their higher academic education costs.
Apprenticeship
A structured employment model combining paid workplace learning with technical classroom instruction.
