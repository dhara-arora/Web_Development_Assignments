Welcome to Apex National University

Founded in 1995, Apex National University stands as a premier institution dedicated to academic rigor and cutting-edge research. Our campus provides world-class infrastructure spanning over 150 acres, hosting multidisciplinary schools in engineering, mathematics, humanities, and business administration.

We foster a student-first environment where critical thinking meets practical application, preparing future leaders for a dynamic global workforce.

Campus Facilities and Student Benefits

Enrolled students gain access to modern facilities designed to enhance both academic growth and personal development:

  • 24/7 Digital Library: Over 100,000 digital journals, books, and international research databases.
  • Advanced Computing Labs: Equipped with high-performance workstation clusters and cloud dev environments.
  • Residential Hostels: Fully furnished eco-friendly living quarters with high-speed Wi-Fi and round-the-clock security.
  • Sports and Fitness Complex: Olympic-sized swimming pool, indoor badminton courts, and full athletic track.
  • Career Advancement Cell: Dedicated placement support, resume workshops, and direct industry hiring drives.

Admissions and Application Guidelines

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Review the admission matrix table above to verify eligibility and course requirements.
  2. Fill in the online application form below with verified personal and academic details.
  3. Upload scanned copies of relevant certificates, marksheets, and identification cards.
  4. Pay the mandatory application fee via the secure payment portal.
  5. Save and print your unique application registration slip for future verification rounds.

Key Admission Terminology

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualifications and percentage benchmarks required to qualify for enrollment.
Merit List
The official ranked list of applicants published based on entrance examination scores or qualifying academic marks.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to deserving candidates based on academic merit, sports achievements, or financial need.
Application Fee
A non-refundable processing fee required to submit and validate an official admission application.
