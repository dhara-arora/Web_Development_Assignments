Welcome to Aura Health Clinic

Aura Multi-Specialty Health Clinic is a leading ambulatory care center dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality healthcare across diverse medical specialties. Equipped with advanced diagnostic infrastructure and an experienced medical board, we deliver personalized consultation and preventive care services.

Patient First Policy: We prioritize short waiting times, transparent appointment schedules, and compassionate care for individuals and families alike.

Our Specialized Medical Services

Our multidisciplinary departments provide comprehensive outpatient services and diagnostic evaluations:

  • General Internal Medicine: Routine health checkups, chronic disease management, and wellness consultations.
  • Cardiology & Vascular Care: Non-invasive ECG screenings, echocardiography, and hypertension therapy.
  • Pediatrics & Child Health: Immunization drives, developmental tracking, and pediatric acute illness care.
  • Orthopedics & Joint Health: Musculoskeletal evaluations, fracture care, and physical therapy referrals.
  • Dermatology & Skincare: Treatment for skin conditions, allergy testing, and outpatient procedures.

Patient Guidelines & Appointment Process

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select the desired department and doctor from the schedule matrix above.
  2. Fill in your personal details, age, and preferred schedule using the appointment request form below.
  3. Submit your request to receive a digital confirmation reference code via SMS and email.
  4. Arrive at the clinic 15 minutes prior to your allocated slot with any prior medical records.

Medical & Appointment Terminology

Consultation
An initial formal examination and medical evaluation conducted by a qualified doctor.
Follow-up
A subsequent review session to assess recovery progress or evaluate treatment outcomes.
OPD
Outpatient Department—care provided to patients who do not require overnight hospitalization.
Appointment
A scheduled time slot reserved specifically for patient consultation with a medical specialist.
