About University

GLA University is dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, and industry-oriented professional education. Our campus provides state-of-the-art facilities and a dynamic learning atmosphere designed to nurture future technology leaders. We prioritize rigorous practical training and problem-solving skills to prepare students for modern technical roles.

Departments

The university features specialized departments designed to support multidisciplinary academic growth.
• Department of Computer Science and Engineering
• Department of Applied Mathematics and Sciences
• Department of Electrical and Communication Engineering

Each department focuses on building deep theoretical knowledge paired with hands-on laboratory experiments and project-based learning.

Academic Programs

GLA University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate degree tracks focusing on emerging technology stacks.
• Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering
• Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Software Engineering
• Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) with cloud computing specialization

Campus Facilities

The Greater Noida campus houses comprehensive infrastructure including modern computing labs, a central library, and collaborative work spaces. Advanced computing laboratories offer high-speed gigabit networking and dedicated server hardware to support complex project simulations.
