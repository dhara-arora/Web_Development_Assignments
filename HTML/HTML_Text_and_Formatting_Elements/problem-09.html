Institutional Regulations and Student Rules

Official academic guidelines, code of conduct, and evaluation compliance standards applicable to all enrolled students.

General Rules

1. Identification Display: Students must carry their valid institutional ID card at all times while on campus.
Failure to present identification upon request may result in denial of entry to academic facilities.

2. Campus Conduct: Maintain professional behavior in all classrooms, laboratories, and common areas.
Disruptive activities or damage to university property will incur a penalty fine of ₹2,000 and disciplinary review.

Attendance

3. Minimum Attendance Standard: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in every registered course.
Students falling below this threshold will face a debarment penalty from semester examinations.

Examination

4. Hall Ticket and Punctuality: Arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
Late arrivals beyond 30 minutes will face immediate exclusion from the exam session.

Assignment Submission

5. Submission Deadlines: All coursework and practical assignments must be pushed to GitHub by 11:59 PM on the specified deadline date.
Late submissions submitted within 24 hours will receive an automatic 20% mark deduction, after which submissions will not be accepted.

Discipline

6. Academic Integrity and Plagiarism: Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, or copying code solutions is strictly prohibited.
Any breach of academic honesty will result in an instant 'F' grade penalty for the assignment and referral to the disciplinary board.
