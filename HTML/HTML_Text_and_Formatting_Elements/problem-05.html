Science Notes

A comprehensive reference page covering key concepts, equations, and chemical formulas across Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.

Chemistry

Chemical Formula: A representation of the elements present in a compound and their relative proportions.

Common chemical formulas with subscript notation:
1. Water: H2O
2. Carbon Dioxide: CO2
3. Oxygen Gas: O2
4. Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4
5. Glucose: C6H12O6

Photosynthesis Reaction: Plants undergo photosynthesis by reacting 6CO2 + 6H2O under sunlight to produce C6H12O6 + 6O2.

Physics

Einstein's Energy Equivalence: Energy and mass are directly related by the speed of light squared.
Formula: E = mc2

Scientific expressions using exponents and units:
1. Universal Gravitation constant units involve area in square meters: m2
2. Volumetric density measurement requires cubic meters: m3
3. Standard Scientific Notation for large numbers: 3.0 × 108 m/s (Speed of Light)

Key physical parameters like kinetic energy are calculated using Ek = (1/2)mv2, where mass is measured in kilograms and velocity in meters per second.

Biology

Cellular Respiration: The process through which cells convert glucose and oxygen into usable ATP energy.

During high physical activity, muscles undergo anaerobic respiration, producing lactic acid and a net yield of 2 ATP molecules per glucose unit.

Genetic information is contained within deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) molecules located inside the cell nucleus.

Quick Revision

Core Concepts Summary:
• Chemistry revolves around molecular combinations such as H2O and H2SO4.
• Physics incorporates volumetric units like m3 and energy equations like E = mc2.
• Biology relies on fundamental processes like photosynthesis and cellular respiration.
