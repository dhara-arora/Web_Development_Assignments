1. Menu Categories

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka Angaara — Cottage cheese marinated in spiced yogurt and grilled in a clay oven.
  • Crispy Corn Basket — Sweet corn tossed with crisp capsicum, black pepper, and herbs.
  • Hara Bhara Kebab — Pan-fried patties made of spinach, green peas, and potatoes.

Main Course

  • Royal Dal Makhani — Slow-cooked black lentils simmered overnight with cream and butter.
  • Paneer Butter Masala — Tender cottage cheese cubes cooked in a rich tomato and cashew gravy.
  • Vegetable Dum Biryani — Fragrant basmati rice layered with vegetables and aromatic spices.

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun with Rabri — Warm milk dumplings served with reduced saffron milk.
  • Saffron Rasmalai — Soft cottage cheese discs soaked in cardamom-infused milk.

Beverages

  • Special Mango Lassi — Chilled yogurt drink blended with fresh Alphonso mango pulp.
  • Masala Craft Chai — Traditional brewed tea infused with ginger and spices.

2. Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow this 4-step course sequence for a complete gastronomic journey:

  1. Begin with a refreshing glass of Special Mango Lassi to cleanse your palate.
  2. Enjoy the fiery smoked flavors of Paneer Tikka Angaara as your starter.
  3. Relish the main course combination of Royal Dal Makhani served with hot Butter Naan.
  4. Conclude your meal with warm Gulab Jamun served over chilled Rabri.
