AURA 2026 — Annual College Cultural and Tech Fest

Welcome to AURA 2026, the flagship annual cultural and technological festival of our university! Join us for three high-energy days of fierce competitions, creative performances, technical hackathons, and inspiring keynotes.

"AURA 2026 brings together youth innovation, artistic expression, and competitive excellence. We invite talent from across the nation to showcase their skills, collaborate, and create unforgettable memories."

— Central Fest Student Organizing Committee

Registration Deadline: October 10, 2026

1. Fest Introduction

AURA 2026 features over 20+ exciting events spanning technical coding marathons, robotics, dance, music, drama, fine arts, and literary challenges. With participants joining from over 50 universities, the fest provides a dynamic platform for networking, skill validation, and celebration.

4. Step-by-Step Registration Process

Follow these steps to successfully register for events:

  1. Visit the official AURA 2026 online registration portal.
  2. Create a participant account using a valid email and mobile number.
  3. Select the competitions you wish to participate in (Individual or Team).
  4. Fill in team member details if registering for team events.
  5. Pay the required registration fee securely online.
  6. Download your unique registration confirmation ticket containing your Fest ID Code.

5. Rules and Regulations

All registered participants must adhere to the following rules:

  • Mandatory Requirement: Every participant must carry a physical government ID proof and valid college ID card at all times.
  • Mandatory Requirement: Reporting time for all events is 30 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
  • Unfair means, plagiarism, or indiscipline will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • The decision of the official event judges will be final and binding.
  • Substance use, smoking, or alcohol strictly prohibited anywhere on campus.

6. Fest Terminology and Key Definitions

Open Event
A competition open to students across all streams, departments, and external colleges without prior qualification filters.
Team Event
A challenge requiring collaboration among multiple members representing a single team.
Individual Event
A competition where single participants compete independently.
Registration
The formal submission of entry details and fee payment to secure event participation.
Coordinator
A designated student or faculty leader responsible for managing event execution and assisting participants.
Final Round
The concluding stage of a competition featuring top-shortlisted participants competing for prizes.
