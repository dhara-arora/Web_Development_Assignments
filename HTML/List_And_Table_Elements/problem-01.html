1. Admission Overview

GLA University offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programs. All admissions for the 2026-2027 academic session are governed by standard merit guidelines, competitive entrance examination scores, and centralized university counselling.

2. Eligibility Criteria

Candidates seeking admission must satisfy all general and course-specific eligibility criteria:

Mandatory Requirement: Candidates must have passed their qualifying examination (10+2 or equivalent) from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate score of 60% in core subjects.

3. Application Process

Please follow these sequential steps to complete your admission application:

  1. Visit the official online portal and register an active user account.
  2. Fill in your personal, academic, and contact details in the application form.
  3. Upload scanned copies of all mandatory certificates and identification documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application registration fee online.
  5. Submit the completed application form and download the confirmation slip.
  6. Attend the entrance examination or merit counselling on the scheduled date.

4. Required Documents

Candidates must keep the following documents ready for verification during admission:

  • Grade 10 Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate
  • Grade 12 Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate
  • Valid Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate
  • Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS), if applicable
  • Government-issued ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / Passport)
  • Recent passport-sized photographs

5. Important Terms

Key terminology used throughout the admission handbook:

Eligibility
The minimum qualification, aggregate percentage, and age criteria necessary to apply for a course.
Merit List
The rank-ordered list of candidates prepared based on academic performance or entrance exam scores.
Counselling
The seat allocation session where candidates choose their program based on rank availability.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to students based on merit, rank, or financial need.
Registration
The formal enrollment process that confirms candidate admission into the university roster.
