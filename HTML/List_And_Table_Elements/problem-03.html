4. Pre-Practical Laboratory Steps

Students must strictly complete the following sequential steps prior to entering any practical laboratory session:

  1. Review the assigned experiment theory and algorithm in the laboratory manual.
  2. Wear the prescribed department ID card and lab coat before entering the laboratory.
  3. Sign in your entry details in the laboratory attendance register at the front desk.
  4. Power on your allocated workstation and log into your designated student portal account.
  5. Execute the program or experiment under the direct supervision of the lab instructor.
  6. Save your code, complete your lab logbook entries, and get them signed by the faculty before leaving.

5. Classroom Rules and Regulations

All enrolled departmental students are required to follow these mandatory rules:

  • Mandatory Requirement: Maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in both theory and practical classes to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
  • Mandatory Requirement: Electronic gadgets must be kept on silent mode during theory lectures.
  • Submission of lab records and assignments must strictly happen on or before the declared deadlines.
  • Food items and liquid beverages are strictly prohibited inside the computer laboratories.
  • Respectful conduct towards faculty, lab assistants, and fellow peers is strictly expected at all times.

6. Academic Definitions and Terms

Lecture
An instructional presentation by faculty covering core theoretical concepts and mathematical proofs.
Lab
A hands-on practical session held in designated computer rooms to implement theoretical algorithms into functional software code.
Tutorial
A small-group interactive session focused on solving complex problem sets, clearing doubts, and reviewing coursework.
Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation of student academic performance through periodic class tests, quizzes, and lab assignments throughout the term.
Practical
An end-of-term hands-on laboratory examination testing practical programming and problem-solving skills.
